SCP: Stop The Insurgency

Trust no one. Find the infiltrators. Protect the Foundation.

SCP: Stop The Insurgency is a point-and-click puzzle game set within the SCP Foundation universe. Your mission is to interrogate individuals suspected of being agents of the Chaos Insurgency, a hostile organization intent on infiltrating Foundation facilities, destroying critical infrastructure, and freeing contained SCPs.

Can you expose the infiltrators before it's too late?

🎮 Gameplay

Interrogate Suspects — Question potential Chaos Insurgency agents and investigate their stories.
Solve Puzzles — Analyze clues and use your reasoning to uncover hidden threats.
Identify Infiltrators — Distinguish loyal Foundation personnel from enemy operatives.
Protect the Facility — Prevent sabotage and stop the release of contained SCPs.
